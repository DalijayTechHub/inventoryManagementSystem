Blinks Inventory is a cloud-based inventory management platform built for businesses that need to know exactly what they have, where it is, and when to reorder — at any scale. Whether you run a single retail storefront, a network of wholesale distribution centers, a fast-growing e-commerce brand, or a multi-site warehouse operation, Blinks gives you a single source of truth for every unit of stock you own. From the moment a product arrives at your facility to the moment it ships to a customer, every movement is tracked, recorded, and visible in real time.

## How Blinks Inventory Is Structured

Blinks Inventory has two main layers you will interact with: the **dashboard** and the **API**.

The **dashboard** is your day-to-day command center. It gives your team a visual interface for managing products and SKUs, monitoring live stock levels, processing purchase and sales orders, receiving inventory, making adjustments, and reviewing reports. Every member of your organization — from warehouse staff scanning barcodes to managers reviewing forecasts — works inside the dashboard.

The **REST API** gives you programmatic access to everything the dashboard can do. Use it to sync Blinks with your e-commerce platform, automate order creation from your ERP, push shipment updates from your 3PL, or build custom internal tooling. Every API call is authenticated with a Bearer token and returns structured JSON. You can find the full reference in the [API documentation](/api-reference).
