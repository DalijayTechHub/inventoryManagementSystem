Selling across multiple online storefronts is one of the fastest ways to grow revenue — and one of the fastest ways to create inventory chaos if your systems aren't talking to each other. The Blinks e-commerce integration keeps your product catalog, stock levels, and incoming orders in sync automatically, so you can focus on growth rather than reconciliation.

## Supported platforms

Blinks currently supports the following e-commerce platforms:

- **Shopify** — Full real-time sync for products, variants, inventory levels, and orders.
- **WooCommerce** — REST API-based sync for stock quantities and order ingestion.
- **Magento (Adobe Commerce)** — Product and inventory sync via the Magento REST API.

<Note>
  Additional platform integrations are on the roadmap. If your platform isn't
  listed, you can build a custom sync using the Blinks REST API — see the
  [API Reference](/api-reference/introduction) for details.
</Note>

## What syncs automatically

Once connected, the following data flows between your storefront and Blinks without any manual intervention:

| Data | Direction | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Product catalog (name, SKU, description) | Storefront → Blinks (initial import) | On connect & manual refresh |
| Stock levels (on-hand quantities) | Blinks → Storefront | Real-time |
| Incoming customer orders | Storefront → Blinks | Real-time |
| Order status updates | Blinks → Storefront | On status change |

Stock levels flow **from Blinks outward** to your storefront, making Blinks the single source of truth. If you adjust stock manually in Blinks — after a receiving shipment or a stock count — those corrected quantities push to your storefront within seconds.

## Connecting Shopify

The steps below walk you through authorizing Blinks to access your Shopify store and enabling real-time stock sync. The process for WooCommerce and Magento follows the same general pattern, with platform-specific credential steps noted in each connector.

<Note>
  Every product that should sync must have a matching SKU in both Blinks and
  Shopify. SKUs are the key used to map products across systems — products
  without a SKU on either side will not sync and will appear in the SKU mismatch
  report.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Shopify connector">
    In Blinks, navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Shopify**. Click the
    **Connect** button on the Shopify tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Blinks redirects you to Shopify's authorization page. Sign in to the
    Shopify account that owns the store you want to connect, review the
    requested permissions, and click **Install app**. Shopify redirects you back
    to Blinks with the connection established. You do not need to copy or paste
    any API credentials — the OAuth handshake handles everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map product SKUs">
    Blinks scans your Shopify catalog and compares SKUs with products already in
    Blinks. The **SKU Mapping** screen shows three lists:

    - **Matched** — SKUs found in both systems. These will sync immediately.
    - **Blinks only** — Products in Blinks with no matching Shopify SKU. Stock
      changes for these will not push to Shopify until a match is created.
    - **Shopify only** — Products in Shopify with no matching Blinks SKU. You
      can import them into Blinks directly from this screen.

    Resolve any mismatches before proceeding, or continue and fix them later
    from **Settings > Integrations > Shopify > SKU Mapping**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable real-time stock sync">
    Toggle **Real-time stock sync** to **On**. Blinks immediately pushes current
    on-hand quantities for all matched products to Shopify and begins listening
    for new orders via Shopify webhooks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How stock sync works

When a customer places an order on Shopify, the following sequence happens automatically:

1. Shopify fires a webhook to Blinks within seconds of the order being created.
2. Blinks creates a **Sales Order** linked to that Shopify order ID.
3. Blinks **reserves** the ordered quantities against the designated warehouse, reducing available stock immediately.
4. The updated available quantity is pushed back to Shopify so the storefront reflects the new stock level.
5. When you mark the Sales Order as **Fulfilled** in Blinks, the on-hand quantity is formally decremented and the Shopify order is marked fulfilled.

<Tip>
  If you fulfil orders from multiple warehouses, assign a default warehouse to
  the Shopify integration under **Settings > Integrations > Shopify > Fulfilment
  Warehouse**. You can override this on individual sales orders if needed.
</Tip>

## Handling SKU mismatches

SKU mismatches are the most common cause of stock not syncing for specific products. To review and resolve them:

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations > Shopify > SKU Mapping**.
2. The **Unmatched** tab lists every product that could not be paired.
3. For each unmatched product, either:
   - **Edit the SKU** in Blinks or Shopify so they match, then click **Refresh mapping**.
   - **Manually link** the two records by selecting the Blinks product and the Shopify product from the dropdowns and clicking **Save link**.

Once a link is saved, Blinks immediately syncs stock for that product.

## Disconnecting an integration

To disconnect Shopify from Blinks:

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations > Shopify**.
2. Click the **⋯** menu on the Shopify tile and select **Disconnect**.
3. Confirm the disconnection in the dialog.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting stops all webhooks and stock pushes immediately. Stock levels
  in Shopify will no longer update when orders come in or when you adjust
  quantities in Blinks. Reconnecting requires going through the full OAuth and
  SKU mapping flow again.
</Warning>

Disconnecting from Blinks does **not** uninstall the Blinks app from your Shopify admin. To fully remove it, go to your Shopify admin under **Apps** and uninstall the Blinks Inventory app from there.
