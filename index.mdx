Blinks Inventory is a cloud-based inventory management system that gives you complete visibility over your stock. Whether you're managing a single warehouse or a distributed network of locations, Blinks Inventory keeps your products, orders, and stock movements in sync — so you always know what you have, where it is, and when to reorder.

## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.blinks.it](https://app.blinks.it) and complete your organization profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your warehouses">
    Define the locations where you store inventory — physical warehouses, stores, or virtual locations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your products">
    Add products manually or import them in bulk via CSV or the REST API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start tracking">
    Receive stock, create orders, and watch your inventory update in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you want to connect Blinks Inventory to an external system, check out the [API Reference](/api-reference/introduction) to get started with API keys and REST endpoints.
</Tip>
